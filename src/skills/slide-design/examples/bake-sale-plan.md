# Example: a plan for "slides about our spring bake sale, for parents"

For: parents at the school. Remember: come on Saturday and bring a cake.
Look: the Bake Sale template (Confetti).

1. Spring Bake Sale, Saturday [date]: cover with the date, time and place
2. Our Goal: £500 for New Library Books: one big number and what it pays for
3. Three Ways to Help: bake something, help on a stall, tell your friends
4. What We Need: cakes, cookies, tables, a card reader, change
5. On the Day: timeline from setting up to tidying away
6. Who Does What: names and jobs, left as [name] for the organisers to fill
7. See You There: thank you and the address

Why it works: one idea per slide, a number on slide 2, a timeline on slide 5, and the facts nobody gave (the date, the volunteers) left as placeholders.
