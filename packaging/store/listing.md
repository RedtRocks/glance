## Description

Glance Viewer brings the everyday tools of macOS Preview to Windows, in a small, fast app that feels at home on Windows 11.

Ask AI: open the sidebar and chat with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, GitHub Copilot, Qwen, Kimi, Mistral or OpenCode about the document, photo or model you have open, signed in with your own account (free or paid) or an API key. Share the whole page or select an area. Tell it what to change, by typing or by talking: it changes, adds or deletes text in the open PDF or photo in the page's own font, size and colour, and moves the text below to make room. Each change shows with Keep and Undo, and nothing is saved until you save. Prefer the company's own website? Open it right in the sidebar.

PDFs: read in continuous, single or two-page view; rearrange, rotate, delete and insert pages by dragging; merge documents by dragging pages between tabs, or drag pages out of the window to make a new PDF. Fill in forms, sign with your mouse, pen or a photo of your signature, and add highlights, notes, shapes, arrows and text boxes that stay editable in other PDF apps. Add page numbers, headers, footers and watermarks, and check certificate signatures from Adobe, DocuSign and others.

Redaction that really removes content, not a black box on top. Remove Sensitive Text finds names, emails, phone, card and ID numbers. Text recognition makes scanned documents searchable and lets you copy text from images. Clean Up PDF strips comments, metadata, attachments and scripts. Export with a password (AES-256), reduce file size, or save pages as PNG, JPEG or TIFF.

Images: HEIC, camera RAW, JPEG XL, WebP, AVIF, PSD, TIFF and dozens more. Adjust color, resize, crop, straighten, cut out backgrounds with Instant Alpha or on-device AI, mark up screenshots, remove location info, and batch-convert many images at once. Make collages, set wallpapers, and scan straight into a PDF.

Office and more: quick previews of Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, including old 97-2003 .doc, .xls and .ppt, plus CSV, text and code, Markdown, EPUB e-books, fonts, video and email (.eml, .msg). Open them in Office with one click when you need to edit.

3D models: GLB/glTF, OBJ, STL, 3MF, FBX, USDZ and more, with orbit, lighting, materials, wireframe and snapshots.

Every save keeps a version you can go back to, and edits save automatically. Touchscreens and pens work too: pinch, swipe, and tap markup to edit it.

Private by design: no Glance account and no telemetry. Viewing, editing, background removal and text recognition run on your PC. Ask AI only sends what you share in the sidebar, to the AI company you pick.

Glance is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license: https://github.com/RedtRocks/glance
