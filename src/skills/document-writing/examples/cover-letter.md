# Example: a plan for "a cover letter for a junior designer job"

Look: the Job Pack template, page 2 (letter).

1. Sender block: [your name], [email], [phone]
2. Date and recipient: [date], Hiring Team, [company], [address]
3. Opening: the role, where you saw it, and one sentence on why you fit
4. What you bring: two short paragraphs, each with one result and a number
5. Why this company: one paragraph on what you admire, in your own words
6. Close: available for interview from [date], thanks, sign-off and name

Why it works: the facts only the person knows are placeholders, every paragraph has one job, and it fits on one page without shrinking.
