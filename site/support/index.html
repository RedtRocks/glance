      Questions

      Can I use Zelle, Venmo or Cash App?

      No. They only send money between US bank accounts, and Glance is made in India. Any card works here instead, from anywhere.

      Can I stop a monthly donation?

      Yes, any time, from the service you paid with. No questions, no hard feelings.

      Do I get anything extra?

      No. Everyone gets the same Glance, with every feature. Your support pays for the time that goes into it.

      Is my payment private?

      Glance never sees your card or bank details. Playto (in India) or GitHub Sponsors handles the payment, and this page doesn't track visits or payments.
